Benchmark results

No public-benchmark results have been imported yet.

Public benchmarks (such as BFCL and τ²-bench) arrive in Phase 6. Nothing on this page is a benchmark score, and the custom support suite on the other screens must never be presented as one.

What every imported result will show

  • Benchmark identity and version: official repository and pinned commit.
  • Task selection: which categories or subset, and how many tasks.
  • Scoring method: official scorer, or custom subset using the documented selection and scoring rules. A custom subset is labelled "derived subset evaluation" and is never shown as an official overall score.
  • Raw report: a link to the preserved raw output.
  • Exact configuration: model, settings and manifest used for the run.

Custom-suite results and public-benchmark results stay on separate views by design.
